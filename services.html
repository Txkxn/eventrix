01 — Exhibition Stands

Exhibition Stand Contractors

02 — Event Registration

Event Registration & Attendee Management

We provide comprehensive event registration and attendee management solutions designed to deliver seamless event experiences. Our services include accreditation, attendee registration, event marketing support, secure payment processing, lead retrieval systems, NFC and RFID tracking, and detailed post-event analytics — helping organisers manage events efficiently while gaining valuable insights for future success.

By combining advanced technology with seamless execution, we help organisers enhance attendee experiences, improve operational efficiency and gain the insights that drive successful, impactful events.

Support & Management

On-site registration, barcode and RFID tracking, mobile check-ins, self-registration kiosks, lead retrieval, surveys and attendee engagement tools — all coordinated by our team.

Secure Payment Gateways

Flexible payment gateway solutions integrated with your merchant account, or managed end-to-end through Eventrix, for reliable and hassle-free transactions.

Post-Event Analytics

Reporting, analytics and actionable data to evaluate outcomes, optimise future events and maximise ROI — from attendee behaviour tracking to lead analysis.

Staff Scheduling

Structured scheduling and workforce management to ensure every resource is assigned and utilised to its full potential across all event activities.
